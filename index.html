Red · Crimson Fan

Crimson Fan

There is a reason crimson silk never goes out of fashion. Long before ribbons and boxes, deep red pleats were reserved for the boldest gestures — fans carried at the theatre, snapped shut to say everything a glance couldn't. To hold one is to hold something that has always meant devotion — not the loud kind, but the kind that stays. Ours are hand-pleated at first light, when the red is deepest and the silk hasn't yet warmed to the day.

Orange · Amber Fan

Amber Fan

Amber fans don't ask to be noticed quietly. They open in full, layered pleats the colour of late afternoon, and hold that colour through the whole of a bright day. In many homes they're carried by the door to welcome someone in — a small, cheerful insistence that warmth is always worth making room for.

Yellow · Golden Fan

Golden Fan

A golden fan spends its whole life catching the light, and somehow that simple habit has come to mean hope. Ours open wide and unashamed, a ring of gold radiating from a rivet dark as fertile soil. Carry one into a room and the room remembers what daylight feels like.

Green · Jade Fan

Jade Fan

Tall, pale-green pleats, each fold cupped like a hand catching rain — the Jade Fan carries a quiet, architectural grace that most accessories don't attempt. Cut from cool silk with no pattern to speak of, only stacked rows of soft colour that arrange themselves like a hushed choir.

Blue · Sapphire Fan

Sapphire Fan

Sapphire fans open in small, tight pleats, each fold packed close like something holding its breath before spring properly arrives. Their blue is the deep, slightly dusty blue of very early morning, and a single fan is enough to make an entire room feel like it's just been rained on, gently.

Purple · Amethyst Fan

Amethyst Fan

Amethyst has a way of slowing a room down. Even before you catch the sheen, the colour does the work — that soft, spent-violet shade that never quite settles into blue or pink. Traditionally kept among fine linens to travel well, ours are cut long enough to open into loose, unfussy pleats that look like they've always belonged wherever they're carried.

Brown · Chestnut Fan

Chestnut Fan

Chestnut fans are for the quieter kind of romance — the sort that doesn't need to be the brightest thing in the room. Their pleats hold the colour of well-worn leather and late autumn light, warm rather than dark. Rare among our colourways, this one has become something of a favourite for those who prefer their elegance understated.

Black · Onyx Fan

Onyx Fan

The Onyx Fan is the boldest piece we make — near-black silk, pleated wide, with a fine pale seam traced at each fold like a signature. Tall and unmissable when opened, it's a fan that doesn't ask permission to be dramatic, and we wouldn't want it any other way.

White · Ivory Fan

Ivory Fan

There's nothing complicated about the Ivory Fan, and that's exactly the point. White silk, a small gleam of gold at the rivet, the kind of fan a child reaches for first without being taught to. We keep ours simple too — no dye, no drama, just the cheerful honesty ivory has always been known for.

Beige · Linen Fan

Linen Fan

The Linen Fan carries a colour that's hard to name — somewhere between sand, champagne, and the inside of a shell. It's the fan we recommend when someone wants elegance without ceremony: soft, warm, and endlessly easy to carry with any hand, in any room.

Gold · Champagne Fan

Champagne Fan

Our Champagne Fan is finished by hand until the silk takes on a soft, metallic gold — a touch reserved for our most special pieces. It catches the light the way real celebration does: briefly, generously, and impossible to ignore. This is the fan we reach for when a moment deserves a little more shine.

Pink · Sakura Fan

Sakura Fan

For only a handful of days each year, the cherry trees let go of everything at once. What's left behind is a version of pink that doesn't really exist anywhere else — soft, brief, and slightly apologetic about how beautiful it is. We've tried to bottle that same feeling in silk: fleeting, gentle, gone before you've finished looking.
