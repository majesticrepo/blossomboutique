About Us

Our story

Blossom Boutique began with a single fan and a wish to make every gift feel unwrapped slowly. Each fan is boxed by hand in silk, gold, and the colour it carries — a quiet, elegant ritual from our hands to yours.

What started as one folding fan, cut and finished on a kitchen table, has grown into a small atelier - but the pace hasn't changed. We still make in batches small enough to check every seam ourselves, because a gift someone waited for deserves to be right the first time.

How We Work

Our craft

01

Select the Silk

Every panel is cut from hand-dyed silk, chosen and matched until it holds the exact shade its fan is named for.

02

Fold by Hand

Each pleat is pressed and folded one at a time over a bamboo frame - no two fans open in quite the same way.

03

Finish in Gold

The guard sticks are sanded, sealed, and set with a brass rivet, then the fan is checked open and closed by hand.

04

Wrap & Send

It's laid in silk tissue inside a gold box, sealed with a ribbon in its own colour, and sent on its way to you.

What We Believe

Made with intention

Handcrafted, Always

No two fans are machine-identical - every one passes through the same pair of hands from silk to ribbon.

Colour With Meaning

Each of our twelve shades is paired with a flower and its own quiet meaning - never just a colour, always a story.

Wrapped Like a Gift

Silk tissue, a gold box, a ribbon dyed to match - every order arrives ready to hand over, no extra wrapping needed.

Kind to the Earth

Sustainably sourced bamboo, plant-dyed silk, and packaging that's fully recyclable once the fan has found its home.
