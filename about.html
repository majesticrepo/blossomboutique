About Us

Our story

Blossom Boutique began with a single fan and a wish to make every gift feel unwrapped slowly. Each fan is boxed by hand in silk, gold, and the colour it carries — a quiet, elegant ritual from our hands to yours.

How it started

It began on a kitchen table with a single sheet of dyed silk, a handful of bamboo splints, and a lot of trial and error. The first fan took a weekend to get right — the pleats kept slipping before the glue set. The hundredth took an afternoon. Somewhere in between, a hobby turned into a small workshop, and the workshop turned into Blossom Boutique.

How it's made today

Every fan still starts the same way: silk cut by hand, pleated one fold at a time, and set on a bamboo frame with a brass rivet. We keep runs small on purpose — usually a few dozen of any one colour — so each batch stays close to the person who made it, not a factory line.

What We Believe

A quieter kind of craft

Slow, On Purpose

We'd rather take longer and get the fold right than rush a batch out the door. Nothing here is mass-produced.

Made With Care

Every fan passes through a handful of hands before it ships — cutting, pleating, riveting, boxing — never a machine alone.

Kept Small

We limit each colour to a small run rather than restocking endlessly — once it's gone, it's genuinely gone until the next season.
