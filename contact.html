Before You Write In

A few quick answers

How long does shipping take?

Most orders leave our studio within 3–5 business days, then arrive within a week domestically or two to three weeks internationally, depending on customs.

Do you make custom colours?

Sometimes! Email us with what you have in mind — if it's within reach of our current silk stock, we'll let you know a timeline and price.

Can I return or exchange a fan?

Yes, within 14 days of delivery, as long as it hasn't been used. Reach out to hello@blossomboutique.com and we'll walk you through it.

Do you ship internationally?

We do, worldwide. Duties and import taxes are set by your local customs office and aren't included at checkout.
